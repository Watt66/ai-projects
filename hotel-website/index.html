Welcome to Whitesands

A Luxury Beachfront Hotel on Mombasa's North Coast

Whitesands Beach Resort & Spa is a luxury beachfront resort offering 340 stylish rooms and suites with stunning views, five swimming pools, the renowned Tulia Spa, and diverse dining experiences that include the famous Thai Chi By The Sea.

Located on Mombasa's beautiful north coast, just 30 minutes from the city centre and 21 km from Moi International Airport, the resort provides a serene tropical setting with easy access to key attractions — consistently ranked among the best hotels in Mombasa.

Things to Do in Mombasa

Mombasa offers a mix of rich history, stunning beaches and thrilling outdoor adventure. Explore the city's diverse attractions and unwind in paradise.

Fort Jesus

A UNESCO World Heritage 16th-century Portuguese fort at the heart of Mombasa's Old Town.

Haller Park

A nature reserve home to giraffes, hippos and a rich ecosystem reclaimed from a quarry.

Watersports

Jet skiing, glass-bottom boat rides, snorkelling and PADI scuba diving right off the beach.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Whitesands Beach Resort located?

The resort is located 14 km (a 30-minute drive) from Mombasa city centre, along the Mombasa–Malindi highway on Bamburi Beach, North Coast.

How far is the resort from the airport?

Whitesands is 21 km from Moi International Airport and 25 km from Vipingo Airstrip. Airport transfers are available at a fee.

Is parking available?

Yes — we offer complimentary, secure and ample parking for all guests staying, dining or attending meetings with us.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 hrs and check-out is by 10:00 hrs.

Which activities are available at the resort?

Water aerobics, Ozone Kids Club, Tulia Spa & Gym, PADI scuba diving, Blue Ocean watersports (including jet skiing) and glass-bottom boat rides.

Which tourist sites can I visit nearby?

Fort Jesus, Mama Ngina Waterfront, Old Town, the Elephant Tusks and Haller Park are all popular excursions from the resort.

Which currencies are accepted?

The resort accepts most major currencies including Kenya shillings, US Dollars, Euros and Great British Pounds.
